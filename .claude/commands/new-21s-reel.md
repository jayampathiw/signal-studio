# /new-21s-reel [concept]

Create a new Wild Capture 21-second tension/survival reel brief (Formula B) and start generation.

**Format:** 21s | 3 scenes | Register: tension-survival | Slot: Sat 07:30 BST
**Goal:** Follows. 1.47 follow conversions per 1,000 views (vs 0.89 for intimacy reels).

**What makes a strong 21s:**

- Clear external threat (implied or present) — predator shadow, hawk, open ground vulnerability
- 3-scene arc: calm → threat detection → resolution OR unresolved ending
- First-person whispered narration adds follow-trigger ("Stay low. The sky is hunting.")
- Unresolved ending drives comments and rewatches
- Pampas fox, hawk, storm, open ground are valid threat elements

**3-scene structure:**

- Scene 1 (7s): cavy in calm state, intimate framing, establishing
- Scene 2 (7s): threat introduced — shadow, sound, movement. Cavy detects.
- Scene 3 (7s): resolution (burrow escape) OR freeze-and-wait (unresolved) — unresolved earns more comments

**Benchmark:** "The Sky Was Hunting Her" → 6,911 views, 38.5% watch-through, 13 follows, +1x distribution, 1.88 follows/1k

**Usage:**

- `/new-21s-reel` — prompts you for a concept
- `/new-21s-reel Pampas fox scent-hunting near the burrow entrance` — direct concept

Creates a `content_items` brief with `format='21s'`, `slot='Sat 07:30 BST'`, then invokes `wild-eye-reel`.
