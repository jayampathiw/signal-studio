## RESEARCH PROMPT (copy from here)

- ~70% of scenes are **single AI-generated still images animated with slow zoom / pan (Ken Burns effect)**

- ~10% are **text cards** (white text on black) for chapter breaks and dramatic beats
- Narration is **AI text-to-speech**, documentary style
- Figures are **anonymous/silhouetted/stylized** (no real player likenesses)
- Target audience: global English-speaking football fans, YouTube long-form

I need an exhaustive, evidence-based research report covering ALL of the following areas. For every area, cite specific successful channels/videos as case studies where possible, with view counts and engagement data.
