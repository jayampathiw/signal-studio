CRITICAL FINDINGS FROM DATA
Before writing any title or description, internalise these:

Tuesday posting killed "One Sip of Water" despite 85% watch-through.
The best SEO package in the world cannot overcome a bad posting day.
The title is the hook — it determines whether the first 2 seconds get watched.
"One Sip of Water Could Cost Her Everything" is too gentle.
"She Has Two Seconds To Drink Or Die" is the same video with a better hook.
Descriptions are almost never read before someone decides to watch.
Write descriptions for the algorithm (keywords) and for people who watched and want more.
Not for people deciding whether to watch — that decision is made by the title and thumbnail.
Hashtags matter less on Facebook Reels than on Instagram.
Distribution is driven by watch-time signals, not hashtag matching.
Use 15 hashtags for Reels (marginal benefit), 6–8 for images (sweet spot).

DESCRIPTION STRUCTURE (Reels)

Scene-setting sentence — orient the viewer, establish stakes
One emotional or tension beat — why this moment matters
One specific detail — something only this video has (a fact, a behaviour, a place)
Location note — grounds the intimacy
Final line (always exact): Follow for more hidden moments from the wild.

Sentence rhythm rule: Vary length. Long sentence → two or three short beats → long again.
See storm reel description as the benchmark.
